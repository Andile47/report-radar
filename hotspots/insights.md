# Hotspot insights

Reports are concentrated in two hotspots: Old Alexandra (1,401 reports) and East Bank (1,037) together account for 61% of all 3,994 reports, so placing field teams and depots near them will cut response times the most, while Tsutsumani is the quietest (236 reports). Water is the most common problem in every hotspot, and average urgency is similar everywhere (3.0 to 3.1 out of 5), so the busiest areas need more capacity rather than different priorities. Fewer reports does not have to mean fewer problems: quieter areas may simply report less, which the Week 6 fairness check should look at (Pan Africa has no hotspot of its own because its reports fall inside neighbouring clusters).
